Fair questions

Asked and answered.

Do I need a membership to order labs?

No. Lab panels can be ordered on their own.

Where do I get my blood drawn?

At a LabCorp location. Your requisition is in your patient portal right after you order — schedule your draw with LabCorp.

How do I get my results?

You're notified when they're in your patient portal, where you can download them.

Which panel should I choose?

Match it to your goal: HRT or TRT for hormone therapy, GLP-1 for weight care, peptide for peptide therapy, or comprehensive for the broadest look.

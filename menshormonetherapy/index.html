Fair questions

Asked and answered.

Do I need labs first?

Yes — always. If you have results from the last 90 days, have them downloaded and ready to upload at the end of your assessment. If not, order the TRT panel first; your requisition is in your patient portal right away and you schedule your draw at LabCorp.

What does it cost?

$149 for a three-month membership, which covers your provider video consult and lab review. Medications are priced separately and start at $23.20/mo — you see every price before you choose. If you need labs, the TRT panel is $149.

Will I need labs again later?

Yes. Follow-up labs are part of safe testosterone therapy — your provider tells you when, and you can order them on the lab panels page.

Do I have to buy what the provider recommends?

No. Your provider recommends; you decide. You choose which medications to purchase after your consult.

Is this the same as the Men's Health program?

No — that's sexual health and hair loss, with no consult or labs required to start. See Men's Health.

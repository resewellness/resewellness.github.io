Questions

Before you start

What is Sleigh the Season?

It is Rese's 3-month holiday program. The Support Kit is the value: weekly check-ins with Sabrina, a welcome call in week 1, a week 8 Plateau Protocol check-in with Sabrina, and a simple starter guide with family-friendly meals. Tirzepatide is one tool inside that kit, not the whole story. A licensed provider decides whether medication is right for you.

Are the weekly check-ins medical advice?

No. Weekly check-ins with Sabrina are short, supportive messages and encouragement. They are not clinical advice. Treatment questions go to your patient portal, where a licensed provider responds.

What happens in week 1?

You get a welcome call in week 1 with a nurse. The assessment itself is about 10 minutes. After that, a licensed provider reviews your information and decides whether medication is right for you.

What is the week 8 Plateau Protocol check-in?

At week 8, Sabrina Mortensen, RN reaches out for the Plateau Protocol check-in, by call or text. That check-in names what is working, what feels stuck, and what you want the end of month 3 to look like, so the week 10 to 12 decision with your provider is informed, not rushed.

What happens after month 3?

Around week 10 to 12, you and your provider review how things are going and decide together what comes next. There is no surprise cliff. If you and your provider decide continuing is right, the next stretch of care is lined up under your provider's direction. If you and your provider decide stepping off is right, you get a provider-guided transition off the medication. How that looks is individual. Your provider sets the path.

Many people ask whether the weight comes back. It is a fair question, and it is one we answer with you, not for you, in that week 10 to 12 conversation with your provider, using your own story so far.

What does the program cost?

The 3-month program is $299 per month. With code SLEIGH, 15% off, it is $254.15 per month through December 31, 2026. One monthly amount covers the program, with no program fee and no surprise add-ons. You are not charged for medication unless a licensed provider approves you.

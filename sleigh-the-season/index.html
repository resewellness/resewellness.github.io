Questions

Before you start

What is Sleigh the Season?

Rese Wellness's 3-month holiday weight care program, with nurse support from Sabrina Mortensen, RN. A licensed provider decides if tirzepatide, a weekly GLP-1 medicine, is right for you.

Is it a shot?

Yes. The medication is a weekly at-home injection. Rese provides education on how to do it.

Do I need labs?

No. Labs are not required. They are available on request for an additional charge.

Does insurance cover it?

No. This is self-pay. We do not bill insurance. HSA/FSA cards are accepted.

How soon can I start?

The assessment is about 10 minutes. If a licensed provider approves you, medication ships within 2 to 3 days.

What if I'm not approved?

You are not charged. Rese holds the card and runs the charge only once you are approved. If not, the hold is released.

What does the program cost?

$594.15 for all 3 months with code SLEIGH (regular $699), through Dec 31, 2026, or $249 every 28 days month to month. Both include the Support Kit. The card is only charged once you are approved.

What happens after 3 months?

We help you move to continued treatment, or a plan to maintain the habits you've built. Your provider sets the path.

Are compounded medications FDA-approved?

No. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished drug products. A licensed provider decides if medication is right for you.
